📖 Format guide:
• EPUB 3 — fixed-layout, pinch-to-zoom support, works in Panels, KyBook, Moon+ Reader, Kobo, Lithium
• CBZ — ZIP of images + ComicInfo.xml metadata; universal support in all dedicated comics apps (CDisplayEx, Chunky, Perfect Viewer, Kavita, Komga…)
• Images are sorted naturally by filename (1.png, 2.png … 10.png, not 1, 10, 2)
• First image is used as the cover
• Contain preserves aspect ratio with letterboxing; Fill stretches to fill the full screen (EPUB only)
